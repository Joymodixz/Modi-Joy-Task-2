# Modi-Joy-Task-2
Analysis of phishing emails

Phishing Email Analysis Report

Objective

To analyze a suspicious email sample and identify phishing characteristics used by attackers to deceive users.

Phishing Email Summary

Sample Email

I used a sample email screenshot that claims to offer a UNICEF Part-Time Work From Home job with a weekly payment of $500 USD and asks interested individuals to contact a Gmail address.

Analysis

1. Sender Email

The sender email appears as Aisha.Mansour@bot.go.tz. The email claims to be related to a UNICEF job opportunity, but the sender’s domain does not belong to UNICEF, making it suspicious.

2. Header Analysis

Full email headers were not available in the screenshot. However, header analysis can be performed using tools such as MXToolbox Header Analyzer to verify SPF, DKIM, and sender authenticity.

3. Suspicious Contact Information

The email instructs recipients to contact thomasnicholasdr@gmail.com. Legitimate organizations like UNICEF generally use official corporate email addresses rather than free Gmail accounts for recruitment.

4. Social Engineering

The email uses the trusted UNICEF name and promises an attractive work-from-home opportunity with high weekly pay to gain the recipient’s trust and encourage a response.

5. Unrealistic Job Offer

The offer of $500 per week for a simple work-from-home position with very little information is a common tactic used in phishing and job scam emails.

6. Grammar and Formatting Issues

The email contains awkward wording and unprofessional formatting, which are common indicators of phishing attempts.

7. Lack of Verification

The email does not provide an official UNICEF recruitment portal, job reference number, HR contact information, or any reliable verification method.

Phishing Indicators Found

* Suspicious sender domain
* Use of a personal Gmail account
* Unrealistic job offer
* Social engineering tactics
* Grammar and formatting issues
* Lack of official verification details
* Possible email spoofing

Conclusion

Based on the analysis, the email exhibits multiple phishing characteristics and should be considered suspicious. Users should avoid responding to the email, clicking any links, or sharing personal information. The email appears to be an attempt to exploit trust in a well-known organization to deceive potential victims.
